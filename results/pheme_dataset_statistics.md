The PHEME dataset comprises 5 distinct breaking news events, containing a total of 5802 conversation threads and 103,212 tweets. Among these, 5,802 are source tweets initiating discussions, and 97,410 are replies forming the conversation threads. The dataset includes 49,345 unique users who participated in these discussions. Regarding rumor distribution, 1972 threads (33.99%) are labeled as rumors, while 3830 threads (66.01%) are non-rumors. This relatively balanced distribution makes the dataset suitable for binary classification tasks. The conversation threads vary significantly in size, with an average of 16.79 replies per thread and a maximum of 345 replies. The maximum conversation depth reaches 47 levels, indicating complex discussion structures, particularly in controversial rumors. The dataset spans from August 09, 2014 to April 01, 2015, covering a period of 234 days. User participation is diverse, with an average of 2.09 tweets per user, highlighting varied engagement levels across the dataset.
